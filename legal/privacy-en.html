Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026
📱
Your data stays on your device only

All your data (sleep, mood, preferences) is stored locally on your device via browser storage only — never sent to any server.

🚫
We collect no data

We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information. The app requires no account and no email.

🔔
Local notifications

Wind-down and alarm reminders run locally on your device only. No data is sent anywhere for them.

🗑️
Delete your data

You can delete all your data anytime from Settings → Reset App.

🤖
One explicit exception: optional AI analysis

If you enable the optional "AI-Powered Analysis" feature with your own key, a computed numeric summary only (no raw sleep logs or dates) is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you personally chose (OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude) — never through any server of ours, since we have no server at all. This feature is off by default, and nothing is ever sent unless you enable it and enter your own key. Your data is then also subject to your chosen provider's own privacy policy.

⚕️
Not a medical substitute

DreamDrift is a relaxation and sleep-habit tracking aid, not a medical app or diagnostic tool. Consult your doctor for any sleep-related health concerns.
